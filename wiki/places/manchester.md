## Public Profile

**Manchester** is a city in the northwest of England and, by its own account and that of most historians, the first industrial city in the world. It built the cotton mills, the canals and the first intercity passenger railway, split the atom at its university and built one of the first stored-program computers there, and it takes the view that every subsequent century has been, to some degree, its idea. It rains in Manchester on about four days in every ten, a figure residents know to the decimal and defend against cities that claim to be wetter.

It is home to two Premier League clubs, Manchester City and Manchester United, whose supporters are divided by a single question that the city has never resolved and never stopped discussing. City play at the Etihad Stadium, in the east of the city. The arguments between the two sets of supporters are conducted with a level of statistical detail that would be recognized as scholarship anywhere else.

In November 2026 the Etihad hosted the Premier League debut of [Magnus Carlsen](/wiki/people/magnus-carlsen/), registered with City as a free agent under an exchange of professions with [Erling Haaland](/wiki/people/erling-haaland/). Kickoff against Aston Villa was delayed eleven minutes while the club's kit manager cut Carlsen's jeans above the knee in the tunnel. In the 14th minute Carlsen offered the opposing captain a draw and left the field; he was cautioned and returned two minutes later. The match finished 0-0. The city's supporters, of both clubs, are still arguing about whether it counts.

## Public Institutions and Fixtures

- **The Etihad Stadium** — Manchester City's ground; the tunnel; the medical scissors.
- **Old Trafford** — Manchester United's ground, to the southwest, referred to at the Etihad only by direction.
- **The University of Manchester** — the atom, the computer, and a long list of people who will tell you about both.
- **The Bridgewater Canal** — opened in 1761; the beginning, Mancunians say, of everything.
- **The rain** — about four days in ten; recorded, compared and defended.

## Private Profile

**What this is for:** Manchester is the universe's city of origin and argument. It invented the modern world and wants credit, and it supports two football clubs whose rivalry gives every event in the city a second, partisan meaning. Anything that happens at the Etihad will be argued over by people who were not there, with figures.

**The exaggeration:** Real Manchester is a proud, wet, inventive city with two famous clubs. Turn the dial until its pride in being first applies to everything, its rain is a point of civic honor, and its football arguments have the rigor of peer review. Keep it warm. Manchester is not bitter; it is certain.

**Generative fixations:** City and United. The Etihad and the tunnel. Carlsen's jeans and his caution. The rain statistics. The industrial firsts. Whatever happens after the international break.
