## Public Profile

**Bozeman** is a city in the Gallatin Valley of southwestern Montana, ringed by the Bridger, Gallatin and Spanish Peak ranges and about ninety miles north of Yellowstone National Park. It is the home of Montana State University and, over the past two decades, of a steady inflow of remote workers, retirees and others who have concluded that the mountains offer a better quality of life and, in a meaningful share of cases, a better place to be when things go wrong.

The city is a regional center for outdoor gear, wood stoves, well drilling and bulk dry goods, trades whose customers it does not always ask about their plans. Its housing prices have risen faster than almost anywhere in the Mountain West, a development longtime residents attribute to newcomers and newcomers attribute to scarcity, which they had moved there to get away from.

Bozeman is home to the [Center for Post-Grid Exchange](/wiki/organizations/center-for-post-grid-exchange/), an institute that models how goods would be priced after a prolonged collapse of the electrical grid, and which chose the city, its founder has said, because a substantial portion of its survey panel already lived within driving distance.
