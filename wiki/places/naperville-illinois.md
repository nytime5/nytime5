## Public Profile

**Naperville** is a city of about 150,000 people some thirty miles west of Chicago, straddling the line between DuPage and Will counties along the DuPage River. Founded in 1831 as a frontier settlement, it remained a quiet farm town until the 1960s, when the East-West Tollway and the research campuses of the Illinois Technology and Research Corridor brought engineers, and the engineers brought subdivisions. It has since appeared near the top of nearly every national ranking of safe, affluent and well-schooled places to raise a family, rankings which residents read closely and cite at block parties.

The city's civic center is its Riverwalk, a landscaped promenade of brick paths, covered bridges and a carillon tower built along the DuPage River in the 1980s. Its residential character is set by its cul-de-sacs, of which it has an unusually high number, and by its basements, which are finished in about the proportion its residents' plans for them are.

Naperville is home to a large population of software engineers, project managers and others professionally trained to identify risk, maintain spreadsheets and escalate. Among them is [Glenn Torrance](/wiki/people/glenn-torrance/), who operates a satirical news website from his home office as a survival strategy against artificial superintelligence and, since 2025, has maintained a commodity reserve of erectile dysfunction medication in his basement against the collapse of the electrical grid.

## Private Profile

**Role in the universe:** Naperville is the American suburb at its most optimized, and therefore the natural home of residents who have optimized their way into unusual conclusions. Stories set here should feel orderly, well-funded and calm on the surface: the lawns are edged, the schools are excellent, and the threat-assessment spreadsheet is up to date.
