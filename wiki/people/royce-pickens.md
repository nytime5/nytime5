## Public Biography

**Royce Pickens**, 61, is a self-employed fence contractor east of [Tyler, Texas](/wiki/places/tyler-texas/), and a client of [Remand Health](/wiki/organizations/remand-health/). On Nov. 10, 2026, he emptied the register at the company's South Broadway Avenue storefront of $812, was taken into custody by a Smith County sheriff's deputy about six minutes later, and was examined by a physician in the Smith County jail the following afternoon. It was the first time a doctor had looked at his right shoulder since he tore the rotator cuff setting a corner post in 2021.

Mr. Pickens has built and repaired cattle fence, pipe fence and privacy fence in Smith County for thirty-four years, working alone since his partner retired in 2015. He has not had health insurance since then. He tried the county's free clinic in 2022 and was placed on a waiting list. In 2023 he went to the emergency department of a hospital in Tyler, which gave him an ice pack, a referral to an orthopedic surgeon and a bill, and the surgeon quoted him $31,000 for the repair. He continued working, building fence one-handed where he could and hiring a neighbor's son to hold posts where he could not.

He enrolled with Remand in August 2026 and waited eleven weeks. At his clinical-legal intake the company matched his shoulder to a Class A misdemeanor theft, to which he intends to plead guilty at arraignment, declining probation. His repair is scheduled for Dec. 2 at the hospital that quoted him the $31,000, by the surgeon who quoted it, at county expense. The surgeon remembered him.

He has described the theft as well organized and noted that the clerk thanked him.

## Private Profile

**Voice and sensibility:** Plain, unhurried, unbothered and without self-pity. Royce Pickens is not angry about any of this. He has spent five years trying the doors he was told to try, in the order he was told to try them, and Remand is the door that opened. He is grateful to it the way a man is grateful to a feed store that stays open late. He says "yes sir" and "no ma'am" to everyone, including the deputy who arrested him.

**Verbal habits:** Measures time by the shoulder ("before the shoulder," "since the shoulder"). Refers to himself, since the surgeon used the phrase, as "the shoulder." Describes every institution he has dealt with by what it handed him: a list, a bill, an ice pack, a note.

**Useful for:** Stories about Remand's clients, Smith County, the jail's medical unit, rural self-employment and the uninsured. He is a natural recurring voice from inside the jail, and later from outside it, when his coverage period ends and the shoulder needs follow-up.

**Open threads:** The surgery on Dec. 2. What happens at the end of the year, when the coverage ends. His other shoulder, which he has started to mention.

**Avoid:** Making him a figure of pity or a rube. He is a capable tradesman who understands his situation exactly and has made a rational choice from the options available. He would build you a good fence.
