## Public Biography

**Erling Haaland** is a Norwegian professional athlete who, until November 2026, played center forward for Manchester City and the Norway national team, and who is now registered as a chess player under an exchange of professions with [Magnus Carlsen](/wiki/people/magnus-carlsen/). He was born in Leeds in July 2000, while his father, the former Leeds United and Manchester City midfielder Alf-Inge Haaland, was playing in England, and was raised in Bryne, a small town on the plain south of [Stavanger](/wiki/places/stavanger/).

He played for Bryne, Molde, Red Bull Salzburg and Borussia Dortmund before joining Manchester City in 2022. In his first Premier League season he scored 36 league goals, a record for a 38-game season, and he has scored at a rate that statisticians describe less as a performance than as a property of the player, like his height, which is six feet four. He is known for touching the ball fewer times per match than almost any other forward in the league and for scoring with a large share of those touches. Analysts have described his style as "vertical." Haaland has not described it.

He marks goals by sitting down cross-legged on the turf, closing his eyes and resting the backs of his hands on his knees. He has spoken publicly about a regimen that includes eating beef heart and liver, drinking filtered water and wearing amber-tinted glasses in the evening to limit his exposure to blue light. His interviews are brief. In one televised exchange early in his career he answered most questions with a single word, and he has not since seen a reason to lengthen them.

In November 2026 he made his classical chess debut in Stavanger against [Grandmaster Vegard Ulvestad](/wiki/people/vegard-ulvestad/). He opened 1. e4 e5 2. Qh5, made 22 of his first 30 moves with his queen, celebrated each of his nine captures on the carpet beside the board and lost on time on the 31st move, ahead by a rook and a bishop with a forced mate in two. The game was officiated by [Ingebjørg Thorsrud](/wiki/people/ingebjorg-thorsrud/), who declined to add time. His appeal, under Law 7 of the Laws of the Game, was rejected. Asked whether he had seen the mate, he said, "Yes."

## Private Profile

**Role in the universe:** Haaland is the universe's pure finisher. He does not build, prepare or explain; he arrives at the target. Put him in any contest and he will find the most direct route to whatever counts as a goal, take it, and then sit down. The comedy is never that he is bad at things. He is usually winning. It is that the frameworks around him were built for people who take the long way.

**Voice and manner:** One word where possible, two where necessary. "Yes." "No." "Good." He is not rude, evasive or bored; he simply does not find questions as interesting as goals, and he answers them at the length they deserve. Never give him a full sentence of self-analysis. Never let him explain a decision. If he must say more than a word, it should be a flat statement of fact ("It's chess").

**Habits to draw on (all public):** The seated meditation celebration. The heart and liver. The filtered water. The amber glasses. The very few touches. His father, a former City player. Bryne. His height, noted by other people, never by him.

**What he won't do:** He will not express doubt, regret or curiosity about the rules. He will not slow down. He will not stop celebrating. He will, if told the rule, comply exactly with its letter and keep celebrating anyway.

**Never invent:** private life, relationships, finances, injuries or anything said off the record. His amplification lives on the field, at the board and at the press conference.
