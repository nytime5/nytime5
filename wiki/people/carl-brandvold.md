## Public Biography

**Carl Brandvold** is a spokesman for the Transportation Security Administration, where he has served in the Office of Strategic Communications and Public Affairs since 2017. He is the agency's principal point of contact for press inquiries concerning checkpoint operations, prohibited items, and proposals the agency has not received.

Mr. Brandvold began his career in federal public affairs at the National Weather Service, where for six years he announced weather, and moved to the Federal Aviation Administration in 2011, where he announced delays. He holds a bachelor's degree in communications from the University of Maryland. Members of the Washington press corps describe his statements as complete, accurate, promptly delivered and unresponsive to the question asked, a combination that has made him one of the most quoted and least informative spokesmen in the federal government.

He first appeared in *The New York Time5* in October 2026, in connection with a [Gahanna, Ohio](/wiki/places/gahanna-ohio/), man's proposal to replace passenger screening with a bowl of knives at every jetway, when he said that the agency "has not received a proposal from Mr. Henderson and does not comment on proposals it has not received." The agency had at that time possessed a copy of the proposal for two days.

## Private Profile

**Voice and manner:** Mr. Brandvold speaks exclusively in negative space. His statements describe, with precision, the thing the agency is not doing, has not received, or will not characterize, and they are never false. He regards the press release as a load-bearing structure and the follow-up question as a load it was not designed for. He is unfailingly courteous and will return a call within the hour in order to decline to comment on it.

**Verbal tics:** "Does not comment on." "Can neither confirm nor characterize." "Refers you to the published guidance," which is contextual. Constructs sentences whose subject is the agency and whose verb is a negation.

**Function in stories:** The official non-answer in aviation and homeland security stories — the quotable wall. He is most useful placed immediately before or after a fact that contradicts him, which he will not have denied, because he will not have addressed it. Pairs with [Dr. Philip Marchetti](/wiki/people/dr-philip-marchetti/), who left the agency in order to say the things Mr. Brandvold is employed not to.
