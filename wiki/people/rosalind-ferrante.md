## Public Biography

**Rosalind Ferrante** is the chair of the Transportation Committee of Queens Community Board 2, which covers Long Island City, [Sunnyside](/wiki/places/sunnyside-queens/) and Woodside, a position she has held since 2017. She has been a member of the board since 2009 and has presided over, by her own count, roughly 340 reviews of street activity permit applications, among them block parties, street fairs, film shoots, two religious processions and one attempt to close a lane of Queens Boulevard for a wedding photograph.

Ms. Ferrante, 66, retired in 2016 after thirty-one years as a dispatcher for the Metropolitan Transportation Authority's bus operations in Queens, a career she credits with giving her "a working knowledge of where every bus in this borough is at every hour, and why it is late." She has lived on 43rd Avenue in Sunnyside since 1987.

Her committee meets on the third Tuesday of every month in a public school cafeteria on 43rd Avenue. Its minutes, which Ms. Ferrante reviews personally before posting, are regarded by board members as unusually complete and by applicants as unusually literal.

In October 2026 she presided over the committee's review of the Queens SlopWalk, a march proposed by [Lowell Pruett](/wiki/people/lowell-pruett/) of [Slop Nation](/wiki/organizations/slop-nation/), and stated the limits of the committee's jurisdiction in a form since quoted in the borough press: "We do not rule on the meaning of words. We rule on whether a bus can get through."

## Private Profile

**Demeanor:** Patient, procedural and immovable. Ms. Ferrante does not raise her voice and does not need to. She keeps a kitchen timer on the committee table for public comment and has never once been seen to enforce it, preferring to let the record show that the limit was exceeded. She regards the agenda as a contract.

**What she cares about:** Bus routes. Sanitation plans. Sightlines at intersections. The distinction between what is within the committee's jurisdiction and what is not, which she draws precisely and aloud, partly to protect the committee and partly because she enjoys it.

**What she does not say:** Her opinion of any applicant's purpose. She has opinions; they are not on the record, and the record is the only thing she considers real. When asked a question she does not intend to answer, she declines in the passive voice.

**Useful for:** Any Queens or metro story that requires a permit, a hearing, a vote or a set of minutes. She is the natural counterpart to [Margaret Liu-Chen](/wiki/people/margaret-liu-chen/), who treats every municipal official as a potentially evasive witness, and who has not yet caught Ms. Ferrante being evasive about anything except whether she has read the written comments.
