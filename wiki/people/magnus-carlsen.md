## Public Biography

**Magnus Carlsen** is a Norwegian chess player, the five-time world champion, and, since November 2026, a registered Premier League footballer with Manchester City under an exchange of professions with [Erling Haaland](/wiki/people/erling-haaland/). He was born in Tønsberg in 1990, became a grandmaster at 13, first reached the world No. 1 ranking in 2010 and won the world championship in 2013. He defended it four times and then declined to defend it again, giving up the title in 2023 and saying that he no longer had the motivation to prepare for a match, a statement that chess observers took as a remark about the world championship and that subsequent events suggest was a remark about classical preparation in general.

He has spent much of his later career promoting Freestyle chess, in which the pieces on the back rank are placed in a randomized arrangement, identical for both sides, before each game, rendering most memorized opening theory useless. He has described classical opening preparation as tedious and has been seen, during long games, walking the playing hall with his hands in his pockets while his opponent thinks.

In December 2024 he was fined and left unpaired for a round of the World Rapid Championship in New York after declining to change out of jeans. FIDE relaxed its dress code to permit jeans later in the same event. He is a lifelong soccer supporter and, in December 2019, briefly held the top ranking among some seven million managers in the Premier League's official fantasy game.

He made his Premier League debut on Nov. 7, 2026, at the Etihad Stadium in [Manchester](/wiki/places/manchester/), against Aston Villa. He arrived in jeans, which City's kit manager cut above the knee in the tunnel with medical scissors; the referee accepted them as shorts. In the 14th minute he offered the Aston Villa captain a draw, shook hands on it, and left the field, for which he was cautioned under Law 12. The match finished 0-0. He has since submitted, through the club, a proposal to the International Football Association Board that the starting positions of each side's outfield players be drawn at random before kickoff. He named himself captain of his own fantasy side and scored two points.

## Private Profile

**Role in the universe:** Carlsen is the universe's bored genius. He can see the result before anyone else, and he cannot see why everyone needs to keep playing once he has. Placed in any structured activity, he finds the part that has been over-prepared, declares it theory, and proposes to randomize it. He is not lazy and he is not a bad sport. He is impatient with ritual and very good at everything that comes after it.

**Voice and manner:** Blunt, conversational, faintly amused, never pompous. Short declarative sentences. He states what "everybody could see" as though it were obvious, because to him it was. He is candid about what bores him. He does not rant; he shrugs in prose. "The position was equal after fourteen minutes. Everybody could see it."
