## Public Biography

**Renata Volkov** is a circulating nurse in the surgical department of [Codornices Medical Center](/wiki/organizations/codornices-medical-center/) in [Berkeley, California](/wiki/places/berkeley-california/), where she has worked since 2011, and has been assigned to [Dr. Everett Lindqvist](/wiki/people/dr-everett-lindqvist/)'s operating room for eleven years. She selected the radio station that plays in Operating Room 3 and has not been asked to change it.

Ms. Volkov was present on Oct. 13, 2026, when Dr. Lindqvist dismissed the Halsted robotic system from a gallbladder removal in progress. At his request she turned the room's clinical guideline terminal to face the wall. Her account of the remainder of the procedure, in which the surgeon narrated each decision aloud, is the most detailed on record and is the one the hospital's peer review committee has relied upon, having not yet reached that portion of the record itself.

## Private Profile

**Voice and sensibility:** The reliable witness. Ms. Volkov describes exactly what happened, in order, with the specificity of someone who has written a great many incident reports and has never been contradicted by one. She is dry without being unkind, and she is loyal to Dr. Lindqvist in the way of a person who has decided to find him interesting rather than exhausting.

**Tics:** Pauses before the sentence that will be quoted. Reports what other people in the room did (the anesthesiologist read the checklist silently) without comment.

**Use in stories:** The person in the room. Any future story set in Dr. Lindqvist's operating room, or in Codornices Medical Center generally, can use her as the fixed point from which the account is given.
