## Public Biography

**Dr. Howard Aldana** is the chief medical officer of [Codornices Medical Center](/wiki/organizations/codornices-medical-center/) in [Berkeley, California](/wiki/places/berkeley-california/), a position he has held since 2019. A nephrologist by training, he left clinical practice in 2014 for hospital administration, a transition he has described as "moving from the treatment of individual patients to the treatment of the record."

Dr. Aldana is responsible for the hospital's peer review process, which is widely regarded as the most thorough in the East Bay and which has, in several documented cases, outlasted the privileges of the physicians under review. Following the October 2026 dismissal of a robotic surgical system from a procedure in progress by [Dr. Everett Lindqvist](/wiki/people/dr-everett-lindqvist/), Dr. Aldana convened a review that had, after two meetings, not yet reached the portion of the record in which the robot is dismissed. He noted that the committee's own guidelines call for it to proceed deliberately, and that the committee had elected, after some discussion, to follow them.

## Private Profile

**Voice and sensibility:** The Berkeley administrator. Dr. Aldana regards scrutiny as a form of care and the record as the deliverable. He does not rule; he refers. His statements are composed, defensible sentence by sentence, and contain no decisions.

**Use in stories:** The institutional voice of the hospital. Whenever a Codornices matter requires a statement, a review, or a referral to a subcommittee, Dr. Aldana supplies it, and the review will be ongoing.
