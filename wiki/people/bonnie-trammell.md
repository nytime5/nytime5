## Public Biography

**Lt. Bonnie Trammell** is the public information officer of the Smith County Sheriff's Office in [Tyler, Texas](/wiki/places/tyler-texas/), a post she has held since 2017, after nineteen years in the department, eleven of them in jail operations. She is the office's spokeswoman on matters concerning the Smith County jail, including, since June 2026, the effect on the jail's medical budget of [Remand Health](/wiki/organizations/remand-health/), whose clients steal from its storefront in order to be confined there for medical treatment.

Lt. Trammell has confirmed that the jail's medical budget of $6.1 million for fiscal 2026 had been exceeded by $1.4 million by November, and that the jail had added a second orthopedic clinic day. She has declined to characterize Remand's clients, beyond noting that they are "the most cooperative population we have ever booked" and that several have arrived at intake with their medical records in a folder.

### Notable Statements

- "We are constitutionally obligated to provide care to every person in our custody, and we do. We are not obligated to provide care to anyone outside our custody, and we don't. I'm told that's the problem. I'm told it's been the problem for a while."

## Private Profile

**Voice and sensibility:** Level, exact and unsurprised. Bonnie Trammell has worked in and around a county jail for nearly three decades and has no illusions about who ends up in one or why. She states the law as it is, the budget as it stands and the obligation as written, and she lets the reader notice what the three add up to. She does not editorialize. She reports what she has been told, and attributes it.

**Verbal habits:** "I'm told." Gives figures to the hundred thousand. Refers to inmates as "the people in our custody" and to everyone else as "outside our custody," the phrase doing all the work.

**Useful for:** Any story touching the Smith County jail, Remand's clients, the county budget or the Commissioners Court. She is the official who can confirm every fact in a Remand story without endorsing any of them.

**Avoid:** Making her hostile to Remand or sympathetic to it. She has a jail to run. The arrangement is, from where she sits, a staffing problem.
