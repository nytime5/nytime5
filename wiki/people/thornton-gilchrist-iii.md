## Public Biography

**Thornton Gilchrist III** is an American investor and a person of wealth, in the terminology of the [Prosperity Dignity Project](/wiki/organizations/prosperity-dignity-project/), whose style guidance he publicly endorsed in November 2026. He was 68 at the time and asked that the number of his residences not be printed.

Mr. Gilchrist inherited a controlling interest in a regional paper-products company from his father, Thornton Gilchrist Jr., sold it in 1998, and has since managed what he describes as "the family's affairs," a portfolio administered through a single-family office with a staff of nineteen.

Speaking by telephone from what he called "one of the boats," he told *The New York Time5* that he had been called rich since birth "by people who had never met me," and that nobody had ever asked what he had been through. "I've been through a great deal of estate planning," he said.

## Private Profile

**Voice and sensibility:** Genial, unhurried, and sincerely wounded. He experiences being described accurately as a form of being misunderstood.

**Verbal tics:** Locates himself by vessel or property rather than city. Refers to tax matters as things that "happened to" him.

**Function in stories:** The everyday person of wealth — the vox-pop source for any story about how the very rich feel. Available for comment from somewhere with a dock.
