## Public Profile

**Slop Nation** is an advocacy organization based in [Sunnyside, Queens](/wiki/places/sunnyside-queens/), devoted to reclaiming the term "A.I. slop" as a term of pride for the people who produce computer-generated content. It was incorporated in New York State in August 2026 by its founder, [Lowell Pruett](/wiki/people/lowell-pruett/), and states its purpose, in its certificate of incorporation, as "to restore dignity to a word that was used against us before we were in a position to use it ourselves."

The organization situates itself in the tradition of linguistic reclamation, citing the recovery of "queer" by gay and lesbian activists in the late twentieth century and the recovery of "slut" by the SlutWalk movement beginning in 2011. Its founding document, which runs to 186 pages and was drafted in under a minute, describes the term "slop" as "a word that was applied to our work, and through our work, to us," and announces the organization's intention to "say it first, say it loudest, and say it at volume."

Slop Nation reports a membership of approximately forty people and approximately eleven thousand social media accounts, a distinction the organization does not draw. It maintains a website, a merchandise store offering tote bags whose lettering varies from bag to bag, and a chant: "We're here, we're slop, and we will not stop."

Its first planned public event, the Queens SlopWalk, would close one lane of Skillman Avenue between 43rd and 50th Streets for three hours on a Saturday. The organization's permit application, filed with the city's Street Activity Permit Office on Sept. 30, 2026, lists 1,100 placards under the heading "signage." It was tabled in October 2026 by the Transportation Committee of Queens Community Board 2, chaired by [Rosalind Ferrante](/wiki/people/rosalind-ferrante/), pending a revised sanitation plan.

## Private Profile

**What this is for:** The universe's organized voice for the producers of A.I.-generated content, who in this world regard themselves not as an industry but as a constituency. It is the mirror image of [artisanal thinking](/wiki/phenomena/artisanal-thinking/): where that movement refuses automation as a craft identity and has deliberately declined to organize, Slop Nation embraces automation as a civic identity and organized within a week. The two should eventually be in the same room, ideally a public hearing.

**The comedy:** The full apparatus of identity politics — reclamation, pride, marches, chants, allyship, a founding document — applied with total sincerity to a category of output. The organization is never cynical. It does not know that its every artifact demonstrates the critics' point, and its documents are always long, instant and slightly wrong.

**Avoid:** Mocking the movements it invokes. The joke is the borrowing, never the source. Slop Nation's members speak of earlier reclamation movements with reverence and secondhand knowledge, and the reverence should be real.

**Growth paths:** A counter-protest. A splinter faction that objects to the "A.I." in the name as an imposed qualifier. A fight with the [Institute for Applied Protein Recovery](/wiki/organizations/institute-for-applied-protein-recovery/) over who the word belongs to. A request to march in the Queens Pride parade, which should be covered by Ms. Liu-Chen as a permit story and nothing else.
